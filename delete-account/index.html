Satoshi: Hacker Wars: Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Satoshi: Hacker Wars account (Android app com.drudley.satoshihackerwars and the web beta), developed by Drudley (Niklas Bergström).

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Satoshi: Hacker Wars.
  2. Scroll to Account at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account, confirm, and type DELETE.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email drudley@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Satoshi: Hacker Wars account". Include your hacker name and, if you have it, your device ID (shown under Account in the app). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your hacker name and profile settings
  • All hack attempts, results, rob records and race entries
  • All test sats and points, which are forfeited (they have no monetary value)

What is kept

Transaction history entries stay linked only to the random device ID, with no name attached, for fraud prevention and accounting integrity. They are kept for as long as the game runs. The device ID is blocked from future use. See the privacy policy.
