Satoshi: Hacker Wars: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 (updated for beta 0.4.0). This policy covers the Satoshi: Hacker Wars Android app (com.drudley.satoshihackerwars), its test builds, and the web version at drudley.github.io/btc-game-web/beta/. The developer is Niklas Bergström (Sweden), who publishes as "Drudley". Contact: drudley@gmail.com.

Summary

The game needs a small amount of data to run online play: a random device ID, the hacker name you choose, and your game history. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising. There is no analytics or crash-reporting SDK and no advertising ID. You can delete your account at any time.

Beta notice

In-game vsats (virtual sats, not real bitcoin) and store credits have no monetary value and can never be withdrawn, sold or exchanged. The ads in the beta are simulated. The game plans a free weekly contest where the top scores can win a prize in bitcoin (sats); prize payouts are switched off in the beta and no payout data is collected. Before prizes are paid, we will update this policy to cover the data needed to pay winners (for example a Lightning address and any identity or tax details the law requires) and publish the contest rules.

What we collect

  • Device ID: a random identifier the app creates on first start and stores on your device. It is your test sign-in. It isn't your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID, and it can't identify you on its own.
  • Hacker name: the 2–20 character name you choose. Other players see it in PvP target lists and race standings. Don't use your real name.
  • Game history: your vsats (score) and store-credit balances and their transaction history (hacks, rewards, race and contest results, store purchases), weekly scores and rankings, your hack attempts and results (including input timing used to check puzzles), store items (firewalls, Exploit Kits, VPN, Spyware) and scan reveals, your online/offline ("cut the cord") status, and race entries.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and request metadata to deliver and secure the service. We don't store IP addresses in the game database.

What we don't collect

We don't collect email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data, or payment details, and the app doesn't request any of those permissions.

How we use it

We use this data only to run the game: show your balances, check puzzle results on the server, run PvP and races, and prevent cheating and abuse.

Where it is stored

Data is sent over HTTPS (encrypted in transit) to our game server on Cloudflare Workers, with a Cloudflare D1 database (Cloudflare, Inc. acts as our processor). It may be processed outside the EU/EEA under Cloudflare's standard contractual safeguards. Your device ID and settings are also kept in the app's local storage on your phone.

Sharing

We don't sell your data or share it with third parties, except Cloudflare as our hosting processor and where required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete your account. To delete it, open the app, go to Account → Delete account, or follow the steps on the account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your name, hack attempts, rob records and race entries straight away and forfeits your test balances. We keep the transaction history only as entries linked to the random device ID, with no name attached, for fraud prevention and accounting integrity. That ID is then blocked and can't be used again. Uninstalling the app removes the local device ID, but it does not delete server data, so please use Delete account first.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, and you can object to processing. Email drudley@gmail.com with your device ID, which is shown in the app under Account. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY). The legal basis for processing is performance of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in preventing cheating (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Children

The game is intended for adults (18+) and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
