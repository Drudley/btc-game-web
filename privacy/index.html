BTC Hack Miner: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026. Applies to the BTC Hack Miner Android app (com.drudley.btchackminer) and the web version at drudley.github.io/btc-game-web.

Summary

BTC Hack Miner doesn't collect, store on servers, share or sell any personal or usage data.

What the app stores

Your game progress (simulated balances, ledger and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising IDs or crash reporting
  • No ads and no in-app purchases
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access
  • The Android app doesn't request internet access

No real money or cryptocurrency

All "sats", mining output, prizes, purchases and withdrawals in the game are simulated play values. They have no monetary value and can't be redeemed.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
